# Washed - Professional Orchestra: conductor and player review

Use quarter note = 139, even eighths, as the reference tempo. The edition is intended for professional musicians, with individual winds/brass and sectional strings. String headcount, room acoustics, and player preferences were not supplied; determine final balance in the actual ensemble.

Timpani uses two fixed pitches throughout, F-sharp 2 and B 2. Check drum sizes and tuning before rehearsal. Cadence attacks should clarify rhythm without covering woodwind or string detail. The final note is marked to ring without a roll; agree damping with the conductor.

B-flat clarinet and trumpets read a major second above sounding; F horns read a perfect fifth above sounding. Double bass reads an octave above sounding. Full-score chord symbols are concert pitch; transposing parts' chord symbols are written pitch.

## String articulation

The score supplies arco/pizzicato changes, selected short slurs, light detache instructions, and some staccato marks. It does not prescribe a complete up/down-bow plan or string/fingering choices. A printed tie preserves pitch duration; it is not a demand to use one bow through every sustained passage. Section leaders should coordinate bow changes and releases without altering the source melody's sounding rhythm.

A quarter of preparation at the nominal tempo is about 0.43 seconds. Extend or relocate a preparation rest if an arco/pizzicato change is uncomfortable. Any such change needs matching score and part edits, then validation.
