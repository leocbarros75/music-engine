# Review with string players

For each passage record:

- Measure range and tempo:
- Source piano gesture:
- Proposed string distribution:
- Player's preferred alternative:
- Reason: register, fingering, bow distribution, balance, articulation, or clarity:
- Comfort and sound assessment:
- Agreed change and source-map IDs affected:

Priority passages:

1. Measures 1-4: long tied texture and triplet figure; coordinate bow changes.
2. Measures 5-8: cello groove and selective bass support; control attack weight.
3. Measures 25-32: dense divisi, accents and changes in dynamics.
4. Measures 33-38: treble-register piano LH redistributed to inner strings.
5. Measures 73-76: octave adjustment and unusual source tuplets.
6. Measures 111-123: sustained lines alongside returning rhythmic figures and repeats.

Staffing: up to 2 Violin I, 2 Violin II, 4 Viola, 3 Cello and 1 Bass simultaneous
lines. Allocate divisi to players/desks; do not interpret stacks as compulsory
multiple stops. Final bowings and page-turn convenience need performer review.
