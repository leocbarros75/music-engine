Instrumentation: Violin I, Violin II, Viola, Violoncello, Double Bass.
Playable as a string quintet or by string sections. No divisi or double stops.
15 measures, D major, 4/4. Suggested quarter-note tempo: 76.

This is an editorial arrangement based on the supplied transcription and the
clarified melodic reading used in the approved piano arrangements. The inner
parts are newly arranged; this is not a literal allocation of every source note.

Violin I: melodic line in an elevated register.
Violin II: harmonic support and occasional passing motion.
Viola: moving inner voice in alto clef.
Cello: bass outline with connecting movement.
Double bass: written an octave above sounding pitch; MusicXML includes the
transpose instruction. No extension below standard low E is required.

Slurs and dynamics are editorial. Repeated pitches under a slur may be gently
rearticulated within the bow. Players may revise bow distribution together.
Professional here describes an ensemble arrangement for experienced players;
the writing is lyrical and does not aim for virtuoso technical difficulty.

Validation performed: complete measures, rhythmic type/duration agreement,
written instrument ranges, slur pairing, bass octave transposition.
Not performed: audio comparison, visual engraving proof, player read-through.

CODE
build_strings.py is self-contained and uses only Python's standard library.
Each part is a list of measures. Each measure contains (pitch, beats) events.
The generator encodes those events as MusicXML and validates the saved files.
To regenerate: python3 build_strings.py
