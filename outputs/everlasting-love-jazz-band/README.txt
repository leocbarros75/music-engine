THE EVERLASTING LOVE OF GOD - JAZZ BALLAD WITH ORIGINAL PIANO

Open everlasting-love-jazz-band.pdf for the full transposed score.
Open everlasting-love-jazz-band.musicxml in MuseScore, Dorico, Finale,
or another MusicXML reader to edit the score.

Individual PDFs and MusicXML:
AS1/AS2 = Eb alto saxophones
TS1/TS2 = Bb tenor saxophones
BS = Eb baritone saxophone
TP1-TP4 = Bb trumpets
TB1-TB3 = tenor trombones
BT = bass trombone
GT = rhythm guitar
BA = acoustic bass
DR = drum set
piano = preserved original piano music, newly engraved

The piano's musical data is unchanged. Layout coordinates and page breaks
are normalized for the combined score. original-piano.musicxml is also
included byte for byte. The arrangement keeps even eighths, quarter = 72.

Read PROCESS.md for musical examples, technical explanation, references,
instructions, and what was checked. The complete generator and 12 tests
are included. No live-player or audio review was performed.

Regenerate in this folder:
  python3 arrange.py
  python3 -m unittest -v test_arrangement.py
Export PDFs using MuseScore and engrave-jobs.json.

All note selections in new band parts are editorial accompaniment choices;
the source work and original-arranger metadata remain in the scores.
